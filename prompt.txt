Build a tiny browser game in a single self-contained HTML file: "Lychee Catch". A basket moves left/right with arrow keys or mouse. Lychees fall from the top at increasing speed; catch good red lychees to score points. Rotten dark lychees cost one life if caught; missing three good lychees also costs one life. 3 lives, 60-second timer, score display, start screen, game-over screen with a restart button. Bright, playful colors, big readable text, no external assets, no network.
